# SwipeShare

# What and Why
This is a meal plan swipe sharing platform. 
Every student who lives at dorm are mandatorily required to purchase a meal plan. It is hard to finish their meal swipes in the plan, unless they dine at campus everyday. Each semester, there are lots of swipes being wasted, and students are selling them near the end of the semester at very low price. It is hard to find sellers and buyers that has time and location fit (e.g. one at Paulson while the other one at Third North, 0.8 mi)

# For Whom
For students who have excess meal swipes and those who occasionally dine at campus and want a discounted price. 

# How
Sellers can list their available time slots, location and price, and buyers can browse the list, schedule or buy at any time. The app will find the best buyer-seller combination based on the location and time. Then they will meet at the dining hall, where the seller swipes for buyer and completes the transaction.

# Scope  

## Why Not Easy
This project requires a database for data sharing and persistence, Google Maps integration and algorithms to match the buyer and seller, so it won’t be very easy. 

## Why Not Hard
In the meantime, there is only one SKU and data models are straightforward. Also, payment will be done by putting the Zelle information, so no any payment integration like Stripe is needed.
